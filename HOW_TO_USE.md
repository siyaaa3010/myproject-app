# 💖 Quick Guide - How to Enjoy Your Gift 💖

## 🎁 Getting Started

### Open Your First Gift
- Click any pink gift box to reveal a photo
- Watch the confetti celebration! 🎉
- The gift lid will fly away revealing your memory

### View Photos
- Click any opened gift to see it full screen
- Read the beautiful caption below each photo
- Double-click the photo to zoom in closer

## 🎮 Easy Controls

### Navigate Photos
- **Click arrows** (◁ ▷) on sides to go back/forward
- **Keyboard**: Use ← → arrow keys
- **Mobile**: Swipe left or right
- **Close**: Click outside photo or press ESC

### Special Buttons (Top Right)
- **✕** - Close photo
- **⬇** - Download photo to keep forever
- **▶/⏸** - Start/stop automatic slideshow
- **🤍/❤️** - Mark as favorite (your special memories!)

### Music
- Click "▶ Play" button at top to start music
- Click "⏸ Pause" to stop music

## ❤️ Favorite Your Special Moments

1. Open any photo
2. Click the heart button (🤍)
3. It turns red (❤️) - now it's a favorite!
4. Your favorites are saved forever
5. See your favorite count at the top

## 🎬 Slideshow Mode

Perfect for relaxing and enjoying all memories:
1. Open any photo
2. Click the play button (▶)
3. Sit back and watch - photos change every 3 seconds
4. Click pause (⏸) anytime to stop

## 📊 Track Your Progress

Look at the top of the page:
- "X of 44 memories unlocked" - How many gifts you've opened
- "X favorites ❤️" - How many you've marked as special

## 💾 Everything is Saved!

- All opened gifts stay opened
- All favorites are remembered
- Even if you close the browser
- Your progress never disappears!

## 📱 Works Everywhere

- On your phone - swipe through photos
- On computer - use keyboard arrows
- On tablet - touch and swipe
- Always beautiful and smooth!

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Open all 44 gifts** to see every memory
2. **Mark your favorites** so you can remember the most special ones
3. **Use slideshow** when you want to relax and reminisce
4. **Download photos** you want to keep on your phone
5. **Zoom in** to see details in photos
6. **Play music** for the full romantic experience
