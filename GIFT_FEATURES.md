## 🎮 How to Use

### Opening Gifts
1. Click any gift box to reveal the photo inside
2. First gift triggers a confetti celebration! 🎉
3. Each gift shows a number for easy reference

### Viewing Photos
1. Click an opened gift to view in full screen
2. Use arrow buttons or keyboard to navigate
3. Double-click photo to zoom in/out
4. Click outside or press ESC to close

### Managing Favorites
1. Click the heart icon on any photo (🤍 → ❤️)
2. Favorites are saved automatically
3. View favorite count in the header

### Slideshow
1. Open any photo in full screen
2. Click the play button (▶) to start slideshow
3. Photos change every 3 seconds
4. Click pause (⏸) to stop

### Downloading
1. Open any photo in full screen
2. Click the download button (⬇)
3. Photo saves to your downloads folder
